It's impossible to consider any living being in isolation. Every multicellular protist, fungi, plant, or animal is embedded in a larger network of complex, tangled relationships. Even the 'individual' is a misnomer, as every organism is an ecosystem unto itself, a holobiont made up of millions of constituent symbionts. Even our very cells, uniform in genetic identity, are made of organelles descended from once-independent organisms that, through the process of evolution, fused into a new whole. This is symbiogenesis, the notion that every whole is greater than the sum of its parts.
